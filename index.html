Risk Framework

Objective Credit Evaluation Principles

We prioritize sustainable financial health over predatory lending. Our credit scoring criteria are explained with total clarity.

Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio

Evaluates monthly debt servicing commitments relative to net verifiable earnings. We recommend maintaining total debt service below 40% of income.

Cash Flow Volatility

Analyzes historical liquidity buffers and stability of deposit frequencies to ensure repayment schedules survive periodic financial shocks.

Payment Integrity History

Values consistent adherence to past obligations. Demonstrating disciplined repayment schedules unlocks preferred interest tier classifications.

Consumer Protection
