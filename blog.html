Math Breakdown

The Reducing Balance Equation Explained

In standard reducing balance amortization, every monthly payment is divided into two parts: interest on the remaining principal, and the remainder which pays down that principal.

As the principal declines, the required interest payment shrinks each month, allowing more of your fixed EMI to pay off the principal faster.

Try Interactive Calculator
Formula Specification
EMI = [P * r * (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Where:
P = Principal Loan Amount
r = Periodic Monthly Rate (Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
n = Total Number of Monthly Installments
Consumer Protection

The Responsible Borrowing Checklist

Ask these four critical questions before committing to any credit agreement.

1. Is this productive debt?

Does this financing create long-term value (education, home equity, business expansion)?

2. What if income drops 20%?

Can you comfortably service this EMI during unexpected career or business downturns?

3. Are there prepay fees?

Will the lender penalize you if you want to settle the balance ahead of schedule?

4. Have you seen the full table?

Have you inspected the full amortization schedule showing the exact total interest?
