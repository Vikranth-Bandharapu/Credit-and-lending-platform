Underwriting Protocol

How Credit Scoring is Calculated

Rather than relying exclusively on historical credit score snapshots, Credora analyzes multi-factor financial capability models to build an objective risk profile.

Cash Flow Stability (35%)

Frequency, predictability, and dispersion of recurring income streams over the prior 12 months.

Debt-to-Income Capacity (30%)

Available surplus after accounting for rent, utilities, and existing debt servicing.

Historical Adherence (35%)

Demonstrated punctuality with utility, vendor, and existing loan schedules.

Mathematical Foundation

Reducing Balance vs. Flat Rate

Understand the financial mathematics that protect borrowers from paying interest on already-repaid capital.

Credora Standard

Reducing Balance Method

Interest is recalculated every month solely on the diminishing principal. As you repay, your monthly interest charge falls continuously.

EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]
Predatory Industry Practice

Flat Rate Trap

Interest is charged on the initial borrowed principal for the entire loan tenure, forcing borrowers to pay interest on money they have already repaid.

Results in an effective APR up to 1.8x higher than advertised!
